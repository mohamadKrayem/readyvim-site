About me

I'm Mohamad Krayem, a developer. This is the environment I work in every day, so it changes whenever something starts to annoy me.

I put it online because configs are easier to learn from than to invent, and I've read plenty of other people's over the years. If a keymap or a file here saves you an afternoon, that's the whole point of it being public.

A word on expectations

This is a personal setup shared for fun — not a product, not a service, and not something commercial. There's no support, no roadmap, and no promise that it stays the way it is today. Use it, fork it, copy pieces out of it. If something breaks for you, opening an issue is welcome, but I'm one person with a day job.
